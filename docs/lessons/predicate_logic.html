#12 predicate_logic

facts + rules -> derived bindings

level 12section iiproof_searchvariable_bindingabstraction

0.215 constant-guess floor
100 samples below

What it generates

Facts + universal rules; answer the *binding* that satisfies the goal.

A two-step chain ``p(X) -> m(X)``, ``m(X) -> g(X)`` is asserted for exactly one entity, and decoy rules fire for the others, so neither "the entity with a fact" nor "the entity named in a rule" is the answer.

english

The entities are: Dave, Frank, Alice and Bob.
You know these facts: Bob is an insect, Dave is an insect, Alice is a fungus and Frank is a mammal.
The rules are: swims implies glows, mammal implies swims and insect implies floats.
Who glows?

Answer with exactly one of: Frank | Bob | Dave | Alice
Reply with the answer only.
