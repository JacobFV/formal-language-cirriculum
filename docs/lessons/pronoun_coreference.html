#22 pronoun_coreference

resolving a pronoun to its one valid antecedent

level 23section iii
2.0 answers per episode
0.5 uniform-guess floor
0.2 constant-guess floor
100 samples below

What it generates

Two entities, one pronoun, exactly one grammatically valid antecedent — the other entity is ruled out by gender, so the episode has a single correct reading. Filler material varies the distance to the antecedent.

Difficulty axes

axislevel
ambiguity2
compositional_depth2
discourse_horizon3
