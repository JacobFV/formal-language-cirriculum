What it generates

Which variant survives transmission across generations?

Four variants differ in how regular they are and how long they are; the stated dynamics reward regularity, punish length, and impose a bottleneck that kills all but the top ``k`` each generation. The population is simulated for the stated number of generations and the survivor is whoever is on top at the end — routinely not whoever started on top.
