What it generates

The listener reports where the explanation broke; name the missing piece.

An otherwise correct explanation has one prerequisite removed. The listener reports the first step they could not follow — that step has exactly one unavailable prerequisite, and repairing the explanation means naming it. The distractor vocabulary is drawn from concepts that are equally unknown to the listener, so "pick something they do not know" is not enough.
