supplementary counting_quantifier

cardinal and proportional quantifiers

level 19section supplementary
2.0 answers per episode
0.5 uniform-guess floor
0.505 constant-guess floor
100 samples below

What it generates

Cardinal quantifiers over a scene: ``exactly k``, ``at least k``, and the proportional ``more X than Y``. The threshold ``k`` is chosen to make the target truth value hold, then the truth is *recomputed* from the scene.

reasoning_depth3
recursion_depth2
world_complexity3
