What it generates

Before answering, say what kind of question this is.

Four statuses, all of which occur: the fact is stated outright; it follows from the stated implications; it is well-formed but nothing in the knowledge base settles it; or it is not well-formed at all because it mentions an entity or property outside the declared ontology. Collapsing "unknown" into "no" and "malformed" into "unknown" are the two failures this measures.
