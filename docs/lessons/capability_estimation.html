What it generates

Of four candidate tasks, exactly one is beyond the described agent.

Estimating capability *before* acting is the point, so the question is which task to expect a failure on rather than what happened. One module is missing and the budget is finite; the single infeasible task trips one of those two, chosen at random, and the other three are affordable and covered.
