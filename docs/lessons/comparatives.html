supplementary comparatives

orderings over numeric attributes

level 25section supplementary
2.6 answers per episode
0.42 uniform-guess floor
0.36 constant-guess floor
100 samples below

What it generates

Orderings read off numeric attributes: bigger / smaller of a pair, the superlative over the scene, and proximity to an anchor. Sizes and positions are sampled without replacement so no comparison is ever a tie.
