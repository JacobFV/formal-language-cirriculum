What it generates

Of four nested schemas, which is the most specific covering all examples?

The candidates form a strict chain (each generalizes exactly one slot by one step up an ``isa`` link, ending at the wildcard), so "most specific covering all" is well defined. Examples are sampled to match the intended rung and to break the rung below it, and both facts are re-verified by :func:`_schema_covers`.
