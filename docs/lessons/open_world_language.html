What it generates

Closed world inside the roster, open world outside it.

The roster names the entities whose records are complete, so absence of a fact about a rostered entity means *false* while a question about an unrostered entity means *unknown*. The three outcomes are drawn uniformly and the world is then built to produce the drawn one, so the epistemic distinction has to be made every episode and cannot be papered over with a default.
