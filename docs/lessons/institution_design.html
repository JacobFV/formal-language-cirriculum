What it generates

Which rule makes the collective outcome happen?

Three agents with different private costs choose contribute/defect in a public-goods game; each candidate rule bolts a fine, a subsidy or a threshold bonus onto the payoffs. Every candidate is *simulated*: the whole 3-player game is enumerated and its pure Nash equilibria found, and the world is kept only when each rule has a unique equilibrium and exactly one of them produces the stated number of contributors.
