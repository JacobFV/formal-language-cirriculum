supplementary long_range_agreement

agreement with the head across attractors

level 20section supplementary
2.0 answers per episode
0.5 uniform-guess floor
0.13 constant-guess floor
100 samples below

What it generates

Subject-verb agreement across a chain of attractor nouns of varying length: the classic diagnostic for whether a learner tracks the *head* of a phrase or merely the nearest noun. Attractor number is drawn independently, so the nearest-noun heuristic is wrong about half the time.
