What it generates

A generated rewrite calculus, and the *next* step of a shortest proof.

Breadth-first search over the whole derivation space fixes the distance from the start term to the goal; a rule is the answer only if applying it lands on a term one step closer, and the episode is rejected unless exactly one rule does that while at least two are applicable. So the label is the shortest proof's first move, computed, not guessed.
