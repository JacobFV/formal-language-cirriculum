supplementary center_embedding

verb of the outermost subject under nesting

level 21section supplementary
2.9 answers per episode
0.37 uniform-guess floor
0.225 constant-guess floor
100 samples below

What it generates

``N1 N2 N3 V3 V2 V1``: the verb belonging to the *outermost* subject is the innermost-last one, so the pairing can only be recovered by unwinding the whole nesting. Depth varies and is recorded.
