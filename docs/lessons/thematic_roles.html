#19 thematic_roles

agent vs patient independent of word order

level 22section ii
2.0 answers per episode
0.5 uniform-guess floor
0.19 constant-guess floor
100 samples below

What it generates

Who did it and who had it done to them. Half the episodes are passive, so first-mentioned is the agent exactly half the time: surface position is uninformative and only the voice marking decides.

Difficulty axes

axislevel
compositional_depth2
grammar_complexity3
reasoning_depth2

The same episode, in each language

english

The sentence is:
  - Frank
  - was
  - chased
  - by
  - Dave
What is the role for patient?

Answer with exactly one of: Dave | Frank
Reply with the answer only.

spanish

La frase es:
  - Francisco
  - was
  - chased
  - by
  - David
¿Cuál es el papel de patient?

Responde exactamente con una de estas opciones: David | Francisco
Responde sólo con la respuesta.

chinese

  - 弗兰克
  - was
  - chased
  - by
  - 戴夫
patient的角色是什么？

请从以下选项中选出恰好一个作答：戴夫 | 弗兰克
只回答答案本身。
