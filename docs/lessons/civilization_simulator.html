What it generates

A ring of agents, each holding a convention, transmitting for G generations under one of two explicitly stated rules; which convention ends up dominant?

The rule (``copy the majority of your neighbourhood`` vs ``copy the most prestigious member of your neighbourhood``) is drawn per episode and written into the observation, so the dynamics have to be read, not assumed. Ring adjacency is given as facts over shuffled agent ids, so list order is not the ring. Episodes are rejection-sampled so that ``answer == the initial plurality`` happens at the chance rate ``1/k``: counting the starting distribution, the obvious shortcut, is worth 0.29-0.37 depending on ``k`` against 0.25-0.33 chance — the residue is the minority of worlds whose dynamics simply cannot be made to overturn the starting plurality.
