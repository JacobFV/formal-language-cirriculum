What it generates

Infer the procedure from its input/output behaviour.

Five named candidates are offered and all five are executed on all three demonstrations; the episode is rejected unless every wrong candidate disagrees with the observed outputs somewhere, so no distractor is eliminated by luck and none is secretly extensionally equal on this data.
