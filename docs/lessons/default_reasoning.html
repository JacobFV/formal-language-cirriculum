What it generates

Birds fly, penguins do not, and nobody said anything about rocks.

A nonce taxonomy carries defeasible rules at several levels; the conclusion is fixed by the *most specific* rule above the individual, and by nothing at all when no rule applies — so the three answers (yes / no / unknown) each require reading a different part of the hierarchy. The target answer is drawn uniformly and the taxonomy is then built to realize it, with an opposite-polarity rule placed higher up whenever specificity is what decides the case.
