comparing two recursive structures

level 17section supplementary
2.0 answers per episode
0.5 uniform-guess floor
0.54 constant-guess floor
100 samples below

What it generates

Which of two bracket strings nests deeper? Both strings are padded with flat pairs so *length* carries no signal and only depth does.
