What it generates

Same predictions, different machinery — until you intervene.

Every candidate mechanism reproduces the observational table exactly: they are *observationally equivalent*, so no amount of passive data separates them. They differ in whether ``y`` is produced through the intermediate ``m``, around it, or partly both, and the episode reports one experiment in which ``m`` is *set* rather than observed. The answer is obtained by running each mechanism under that ``do``, which is the whole content of the distinction between a predictive law and a causal mechanism.
