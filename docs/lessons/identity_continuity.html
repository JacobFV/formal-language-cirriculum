What it generates

Do these two descriptions pick out the same entity?

Renaming preserves identity, replacing more than ``k`` parts does not, merging makes something new and splitting leaves identity with the larger fragment. The rules are stated in the episode and applied by a simulator that tracks identity tokens through the whole history, so the Ship of Theseus has an answer here rather than an opinion.
