What it generates

Carry a law from a mapped domain into one whose edges were never observed.

The correspondence is given; the second domain's relations are not. The only way to answer is to pull the query back through the mapping, apply the law learned in the source domain, and push the result forward again — the minimal, exactly checkable form of reusing a theory.
