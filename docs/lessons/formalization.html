What it generates

Informal claim in, formal structure out.

The formal claim is drawn first and rendered into English by a fixed realizer, so the intended reading is exact rather than a matter of taste. The distractors differ from it by one decision each — the quantifier, the polarity, the direction of the predication, or the predicate itself — so every one of them is a sentence the learner must be able to tell apart.
