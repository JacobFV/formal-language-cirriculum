#24 ellipsis

recovering elided structure

level 24section iiirecursive_syntaxvariable_binding
6.0 answers per episode
0.167 uniform-guess floor
0.11 constant-guess floor
100 samples below

What it generates

Recover elided structure: gapping and VP-ellipsis.

``alice likes tea and bob coffee`` (the verb is gapped: what does bob do?) and ``alice likes tea and bob does too`` (the VP is elided: what does bob like?). Distractor clauses precede the antecedent so the answer is never the only verb or the only object in the discourse.
