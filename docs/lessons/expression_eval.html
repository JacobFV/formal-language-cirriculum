supplementary expression_eval

compositional evaluation of a nested expression

level 18section supplementary
5.0 answers per episode
0.2 uniform-guess floor
0.075 constant-guess floor
100 samples below

What it generates

Evaluate a nested arithmetic expression. The value is computed alongside the tree, so the label is exact by construction rather than by a checker.

Difficulty axes

axislevel
compositional_depth4
reasoning_depth3
recursion_depth4
