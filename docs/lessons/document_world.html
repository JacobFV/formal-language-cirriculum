What it generates

Employment history in one passage, geography in another, a lure in a third.

No passage contains the answer. The employment passage names no city, the geography passage names no person, and a travel passage attaches the person to a city that is *not* the answer — so a reader that grabs the nearest person-city co-occurrence is wrong on purpose. The employment passage also has to be reconciled over time: the person joined one employer, left it, and joined another.
