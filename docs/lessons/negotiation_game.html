What it generates

Alternating offers under stated reservation values, simulated to a close.

Each party values the items differently and accepts the first offer worth at least its reservation value. The transcript is a sequence of proposals; the question is which one closed the deal — which requires scoring every offer from the *responder's* side, not the proposer's, and stopping at the first acceptance rather than at the best offer on the table. Deadlock ("none") is one of the four outcomes and is drawn as often as any other.
