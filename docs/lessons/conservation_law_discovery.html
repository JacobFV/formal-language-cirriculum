What it generates

Find the quantity the dynamics do not touch.

The trajectory is generated by taking steps inside the null space of a randomly chosen weight vector, so ``w·state`` is conserved *by construction* rather than approximately; the five candidate expressions are then evaluated at every state on the trajectory and the episode is discarded unless exactly one of them is constant. The rivals include other linear combinations, which is the case that matters: a quantity that happens to be constant for two steps is not a conservation law.
