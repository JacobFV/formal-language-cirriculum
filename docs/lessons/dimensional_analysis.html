What it generates

One of these four equations cannot be true whatever the constants are.

Quantities carry symbolic dimensions given in a table; the candidate equations are built as products of powers, sometimes with a sum, and their left-hand quantities are *defined* to have whatever dimension makes them consistent — except for one, which is off by a perturbation, or which adds two terms of different dimension. Every name is a nonce, so no physics is recalled, only algebra over exponent vectors.
