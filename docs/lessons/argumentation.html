What it generates

Claims and attacks form a graph; status is the grounded extension.

Attack edges are sampled with cycles allowed, so all three statuses actually occur, and the target status is drawn first and the graph resampled until it realizes it — a claim is not accepted because it is attacked least, but because its attackers are themselves defeated.
