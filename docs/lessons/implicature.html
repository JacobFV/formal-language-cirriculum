scalar inference from an explicit speaker policy

What it generates

A stated speaker policy makes the scalar inference exactly determined.

The scale is a fresh set of nonce words meaning ``at least t`` for every ``t`` in ``0..n``, and the policy — given explicitly in the observation — is that the speaker utters the *strongest true* term. The literal content of the utterance leaves ``n - t + 1`` counts open; the implicature ("they would have said the stronger word if it were true") pins the count to exactly one.
