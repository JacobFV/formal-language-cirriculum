What it generates

How many readings does the evidence leave open?

A novel word has several senses licensed by the episode's lexicon, and the scene realizes some of them. Committing to one referent is exactly the error the lesson is built to catch, so the answer is the *size* of the admissible set — and it is greater than one most of the time.
