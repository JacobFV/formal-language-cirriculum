What it generates

Two domains, one structure: find the element that corresponds.

The second domain is literally the image of the first under a permutation the generator drew, and the graph is rejected unless its automorphism group is trivial — so exactly one correspondence is consistent with the edges and the answer is the permutation's value, computable by anyone who finds the isomorphism.
