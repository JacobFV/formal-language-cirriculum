What it generates

First- and second-order belief over a moving object.

An agent's belief is the last move it witnessed; what ``a`` believes ``b`` believes is fixed by the moves ``a`` could have seen ``b`` see, i.e. the smaller of their two horizons. Both rules are stated symbolically in the observation so the target is exactly determined, and the belief queries disagree with the truth whenever an agent left early — which is most episodes.
