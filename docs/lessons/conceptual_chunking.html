What it generates

Which macro removes the most steps from a library of plans?

Naming a fragment costs its length once and saves ``len - 1`` steps at every non-overlapping occurrence, so a fragment used twice is usually not worth a name. Savings are recomputed from the final plans (planting can create accidental occurrences), and the winner must be the unique maximum and strictly positive.
