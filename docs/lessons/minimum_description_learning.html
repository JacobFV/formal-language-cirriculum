What it generates

The explicit MDL trade: theory length plus the cost of listing its exceptions.

A rule set that covers everything is long; a short one leaves exceptions that must be paid for one by one. Because the exception price varies from episode to episode, neither the shortest theory nor the most accurate one wins reliably — the total has to be summed.
