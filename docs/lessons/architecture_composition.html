What it generates

Compose typed modules into the cheapest pipeline, and name its first stage.

Modules are typed converters over representation stages, and two of them skip a stage at a price. Cheapest is therefore a shortest-path computation rather than a per-layer minimum, and picking the cheapest first module greedily is frequently wrong.
