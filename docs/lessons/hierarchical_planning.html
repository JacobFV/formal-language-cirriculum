What it generates

Abstract actions expand into primitives; only one expansion executes.

Each candidate method is a sequence of abstract subtasks, each of which expands into primitive operators with preconditions and effects. Executing the full expansion from the initial state decides the question: exactly one method both never violates a precondition and reaches the goal, and the other three are re-simulated to confirm they fail.
