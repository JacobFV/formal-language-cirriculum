What it generates

Egocentric and allocentric frames in the same lesson.

Four objects sit one per compass direction from the agent, so every allocentric direction denotes exactly one object; the egocentric episodes ask for ``left/right/front/behind``, which denote only once the agent's ``facing`` is composed with the frame. The ``frame`` field makes which of the two is in force recoverable from the observation.
