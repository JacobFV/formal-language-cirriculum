What it generates

Obligation, permission or prohibition — under conflicting norms.

Norms carry conditions and an explicit, total priority order. Several applicable norms disagree about the queried action and several inapplicable ones (their condition is false in this situation) sit at *higher* priority as decoys, so the answer is decided by the highest-priority norm whose condition actually holds.
