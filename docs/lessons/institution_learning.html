What it generates

Infer the decision rule of an institution from the proposals it passed.

Members have roles and voting weights; four candidate constitutions are stated parametrically (a plain threshold, a weighted threshold, unanimity, a role whose assent is required). Exactly one reproduces the outcome of every recorded proposal — the others are refuted by at least one vote record.
