What it generates

Reorganize a symbolic memory without losing a single derivable fact.

Five candidate knowledge bases are offered with their statement counts. One over-generalizes — a rule that is shorter than the facts it replaces but licenses a fact that was never true — and it is the shortest candidate in the list, so length alone picks the wrong answer. The closure of each candidate has to be computed and compared against the facts that must be preserved.
