What it generates

Is the obligation inactive, active, fulfilled, breached or cancelled?

A promise creates a future obligation the moment its trigger event occurs, to be discharged within a deadline, and a cancellation clause can void it in between. The timeline is generated to aim at a status and the status is then *recomputed* from the timeline, so the label is what the events actually imply rather than what they were built to imply.
