What it generates

``x said p`` is a separate symbol from ``p``.

Two of the four sources are declared unreliable in the episode, so a report is evidence only when its origin is. The five statuses partition the cases: a proposition can be established, refuted, contested between reliable sources, merely reported by unreliable ones, or never mentioned at all — and only the last two are distinguishable by looking at who spoke.
